Join Team Voltage

Tell us about your interests and availability so we can welcome you to the shop and match you with a subsystem mentor.

How to join

Just submit the join form and we will email you our meeting calendar, onboarding nights, and ways guardians can stay in the loop.

Complete join form

What happens next?

Once the form is submitted, the leadership team will:

  • • Email you the Monday build schedule and campus directions.
  • • Invite guardians to our REMIND channel and newsletter.
  • • Schedule your safety orientation and subsystem tours.

Have questions before joining? Reach out at teamvoltage386@gmail.com and we will connect you with the captains.
