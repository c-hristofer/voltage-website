Kickoff weekend launched our REBUILT prototyping sprint with six parallel pods focused on drivetrain, acquisition, climb, software, scouting, and field build. Each pod now owns a milestone delivery:

1. **Drivetrain** finalized a lighter swerve module machining process that cuts assembly time by 35%.
2. **Acquisition** validated three intake geometries using fiberboard mockups and a full-scale driver station test.
3. **Programming** merged the new pose-estimation stack with Limelight tagging so we can recycle autonomous paths mid-event.

Mentors are also guiding freshman onboarding through nightly seminars on CAD, bumpers, and outreach media. If you want in on the action, [fill out the join form](https://forms.gle/2iBqZrgxPvtbfUEZ9) so we can plug you into a pod before subsystem locks.
