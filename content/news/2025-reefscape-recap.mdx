We traveled north with a drivetrain tuned for reef traversal and a scouting crew eager to put our tablet-based workflow to the test. By finals, those hours of iteration paid off—Voltage 386 swept the Tallahassee Regional alongside our alliance partners and brought home the **Industrial Design Award** for the robot’s serviceable modular superstructure.

Highlights from the weekend:

- 6 proud rookies earned their first competition pins while contributing on the drive, safety, and media crews.
- Every autonomous routine logged telemetry to our new Firebase pipeline so the strategy squad could recommend tweaks between matches.
- Long-time mentor **Joe Bernier** was recognized as a Dean’s List Nomination support lead for Daniella, who advanced to finalist status.

Next up is robot refinement before shipping to Orlando—expect a faster intake release and easier pre-match checks thanks to improved pit layouts.
