FIRST LEGO League teams challenge students ages 9 to 14 to solve scientific problems and build autonomous LEGO robots while developing teamwork, responsibility, and time management.

Team Voltage has mentored FLL team #54375 Little Volt 7 for more than five years. Starting in 2023, we began mentoring three additional local teams: #49997 Coral Coders, #54615 Undersea Empire, and #58761 Seas the Future. In 2024, Voltage members helped create team #68786, the Green Goblin Galactic Guardians.

Since 2011, we have hosted the FLL Voltage Challenge. In 2022, 17 teams participated; in 2024, the event moved to Rockledge High School and hosted 48 teams in one day. Voltage students, alumni, and mentors run the event so younger students can experience a professional, welcoming robotics competition.
