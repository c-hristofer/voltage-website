Since 2011, Team Voltage has served as the backbone of the local FIRST LEGO League qualifier. Students run field reset, queuing, judging escorts, and live streaming, while mentors inspect robots and keep schedules running. Hosting FLL accelerates our outreach metrics and introduces hundreds of families to the pathway from Explore to FRC.

Key program goals:

- Equip each team with a Voltage student mentor who can answer build and coding questions.
- Provide a professional event experience with scoring displays, emcee support, and sponsor signage.
- Capture media and testimonials for Brevard Schools Foundation and district leadership to sustain funding.
