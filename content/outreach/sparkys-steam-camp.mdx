Campers rotate through hands-on engineering challenges—robot battle tournaments, submarines, tie dye labs, solar ovens, rockets, spaghetti bridges, and more. Each activity maps to a FIRST core value and gives our students a chance to practice leadership while sparking curiosity for younger kids.

Questions about registration or scholarships? Email **teamvoltage386@gmail.com** and our camp coordinators will share upcoming session dates.
