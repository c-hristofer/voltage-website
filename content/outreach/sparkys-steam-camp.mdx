Team Voltage has hosted Sparky's Stupendous STEAM Camp since 2022 to encourage elementary and middle school students to explore STEAM. Campers get a hands-on introduction to robotics through interactive activities and real engineering challenges, using tools like Snap Circuits and Tinker CAD to learn fundamental STEM concepts in an approachable way.

Favorite activities from past camps include robotic arms, binary bracelets, Harry Potter wands, and driving our competition robot. Questions about registration or scholarships? Email **teamvoltage386@gmail.com** and our camp coordinators will share upcoming session dates.
