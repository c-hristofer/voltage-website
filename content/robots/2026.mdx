Our 2026 build blends field-proven concepts with fresh manufacturing experiments. The chassis is built from pocketed 2x1 rails and features removable corner modules so pit crews can swap a swerve pod in under five minutes. Above the drivetrain sits a telescoping spine that supports both the intake and scoring arm; this architecture keeps the center of gravity low without compromising extension height.

Programming is leaning heavily on AdvantageKit logging so we can compare autonomous cycle efficiency between Orlando and South Florida. A new TypeScript-based scouting portal mirrors that telemetry in the stands, empowering the strategy team to coach alliances with real numbers.

We are still tuning the climber’s soft limits and need more driver practice reps. Hit the join form if you want to help us finish the charge for Orlando.
