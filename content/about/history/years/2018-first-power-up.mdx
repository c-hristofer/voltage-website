 [![Picture](/history/2018-first-power-up/first-frc18-powerup-stack.jpg)](#) The 2018 challenge was based off of old-school arcade games. Both alliances are trapped in the game and must get the highest score and defeat the boss to escape. To do this, teams must maintain control of a scale and switches by stacking on power cubes.
