### 2011 Logomotion

 [![Picture](/history/2011-logomotion/frc-logo-motion_1_orig.jpg)](#) LOGO MOTIONâ¢ is played by two competing alliances on a flat 27' x 54' foot field. Each alliance consists of three robots. They compete to hang as many inflated plastic shapes (triangles, circles, and squares) on their grids as they can during a 2 minute and 15 second match. The higher the teams hang their game pieces on their scoring grid, the more points their alliance receives.
